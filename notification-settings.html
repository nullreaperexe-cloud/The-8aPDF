Get new PDFs and school announcements on this device even when the website is closed.

Checking this device…

Allow notifications when your browser asks. The server checks for new published PDFs and announcements every minute, even when nobody has the website open. Delivery also depends on your connection and phone settings.

To verify background delivery, choose “Test after closing the website”, then close all 8aPDF tabs. The server sends the test in about 30–90 seconds.

On iPhone/iPad (iOS 16.4 or later): Share → Add to Home Screen, open 8aPDF from that icon, then enable notifications here.

Lock-screen alerts: In your phone’s notification settings, enable Lock Screen alerts and sounds for 8aPDF (iPhone Home Screen app) or your browser (Android). Focus / Do Not Disturb and battery restrictions can affect alerts. The website cannot change these phone settings.

Friends Chat read memory

Read markers are saved for your device’s chat identity. Reading messages clears their count; your own messages are never counted.
